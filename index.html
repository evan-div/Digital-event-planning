A recorded keynote. A livestreamed session. A studio photo shoot. One team builds all three under one roof.

Digital and hybrid events ask for a different kind of production than a single night on a stage. Sessions get recorded ahead of time, streamed live to a remote audience, or both, and the visuals still need to hold up on camera. In The Event runs a dedicated studio in Salt Lake City built for exactly that: multi-camera recording, full live streaming production, a photography studio, and a rental inventory of sets, furniture, and decor to match.

Whether you are producing a single keynote or a full slate of breakout sessions, our team designs, builds, and runs the room so your content is the only thing your audience notices.

Recording

Visit our exclusive studio to film professional presentations, marketing content, or instructional videos. Recorded media gives you room for retakes, embedded video, and extra editing polish. Record keynote and breakout speakers ahead of time: a strong option for your next digital event, hybrid event, or any presentation you want to elevate.

  • Multi-camera recording setup
  • Retakes and in-studio editing tools
  • Embedded video and graphics support
  • Ideal for keynotes and breakout sessions

Live Streaming

In The Event offers full live streaming production from our Salt Lake City studio. Design your stream with professional stage sets and customizable backdrops. Our A/V technicians are experienced broadcasters across all major live streaming platforms and are onsite to keep every stream running smoothly, whether you use our studio or your own venue. Services include multi-camera angles, sound balancing, and complete video and stream management.

  • Multi-camera live production
  • Custom stage sets and backdrops
  • Broadcasts to major streaming platforms
  • On-site or in-studio support

Photography

Book our photography studio for product photos, business headshots, or senior portraits. Enjoy private studio time and access to a wide inventory of backdrops, props, and lighting. Our technicians help with set changes to maximize your time in studio, or work with our design team to build a set from scratch.

  • Private studio time
  • Backdrops, props, and lighting on hand
  • Technician support for set changes
  • Custom set design available

Set, Decor & Furniture

As with every In The Event experience, your time in studio is completely customizable. Choose from our inventory of on-trend furniture, props, and decor. Display items like clothing racks and mannequins are available, along with custom printed or branded backdrops. Schedule a pre-shoot consultation with our design team to build a set that works for you.

  • On-trend furniture, props, and decor
  • Clothing racks and display mannequins
  • Custom printed or branded backdrops
  • Pre-shoot design consultations

Digital Event Planning FAQs

What digital event production services does In The Event offer in Salt Lake City?

We offer in-studio recording, full live streaming production, photography, and a rental inventory of sets, furniture, and decor, all from our Salt Lake City studio.

Can you stream to multiple platforms at once?

Yes. Our A/V technicians are experienced broadcasting across all major live streaming platforms and can manage multi-camera streams to more than one destination at a time.

Do I need to use your studio, or can you stream from our venue?

Either works. We can produce your livestream in our studio or bring our production team and equipment to your venue.

Can you record our keynote or breakout speakers ahead of the event?

Yes. Recording sessions in advance is a popular option for digital and hybrid events, and it gives you room for retakes and extra editing polish before the event airs.

What if I only need photography, not video?

Our photography studio is available on its own for product photos, headshots, or portraits, with the same access to backdrops, props, and lighting.

Do you serve clients outside Salt Lake City?

Yes. We produce digital and hybrid events throughout Utah, including Murray, Park City, Provo, Ogden, and the greater Wasatch Front.

Tell us about your recording, livestream, or photography needs. In The Event handles everything from concept to close.
